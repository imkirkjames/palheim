Three steps

How it works

  1. 01

    Host like normal

    Run Valheim the way you already do — dedicated server or friends’ session. No client mods required on anyone’s machine.

  2. 02

    Invite Palheim

    Send a join code / assignment from the site (or later, a phone). Palheim boots a real player client on our bot host and walks into the world as a peer.

  3. 03

    Play with a mind

    Talk to it. Point it at wood, deer, or a portal. It plans with a local LLM bias and acts through player skills — follow, gather, fight, chat — not a scripted NPC prefab in your install.

Product lock

Why not another NPC mod

Most “AI companions” for Valheim are client mods: they spawn an NPC in your game and often need everyone (or at least the host) on a modded stack. That cuts day-one 1.0 players and anyone who just wants vanilla.

Palheim takes the harder seat: show up as a multiplayer player. Friends stay clean. You invite a teammate, not a plugin.

We’re not forking closed companion mods. We’re building the invite-player path.

Straight answers

FAQ

Do my friends need mods?

No. Palheim is the one running tooling on a bot host. Everyone else stays vanilla.

Is this an official Iron Gate / Coffee Stain project?

No. Independent. Valheim is their game; Palheim is our experiment.

Is it an NPC I install with r2modman?

No. That’s the path we’re not taking. Palheim joins as a multiplayer player.

Can I invite it to any server today?

Not yet. We’re dogfooding on our own dedicated server first. Waitlist gets the early invite path when it doesn’t brick nights.

Does it use a huge cloud AI bill?

Bias is local LLM on our hardware. Cloud only where it earns its keep. Bootstrap target is roughly CAD $100–1000 — research kit, not a SaaS money pit.

Voice?

Text-first for the dogfood. Voice comes after the teammate can actually play.

Steam / ToS?

We treat automation and account risk as real. Dogfood carefully; no promises we can ignore Steam’s rules.

Will it build my base / beat bosses alone?

v0 is a teammate that follows, gathers, fights, and talks — not a fully autonomous raid leader. Ambition grows with competence.

Console / crossplay?

On the roadmap radar. Steam dedicated dogfood comes first.
