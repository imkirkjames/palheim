Why not another NPC mod

Most “AI companions” for Valheim are client mods: they spawn an NPC in your game and often need a modded stack. That cuts day-one 1.0 players.

Palheim takes the harder seat: show up as a multiplayer player. Friends stay clean. You invite a teammate, not a plugin.
